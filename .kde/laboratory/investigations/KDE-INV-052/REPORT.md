**Observation**: Seeds and engines are not just different components—they represent different **types** in a type system:
- **Seeds**: Immutable types (cannot change)

- Self-referential validation (investigations validate themselves)

**Observation**: Evidence hierarchy is not just about importance—it's about **provenance tracking**:
