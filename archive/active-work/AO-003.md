### Binding owner-approved float policy (V3 and V4)
- **Reject NaN, +Infinity, -Infinity before transmission and before any outstation handler side effect**, on the public bridge and internal Master/Outstation paths where applicable. Do not coerce an invalid value to zero or SUCCESS. If an inbound wire NaN/Inf arrives, reject without a callback or successful response. The policy is for AO-003 command outputs, not unrelated DNP3 analog measurements.
- **Finite values are allowed**, including negative/positive zero, smallest positive and negative subnormals, normal finite extremes and negative values; preserve their IEEE-754 representation without unapproved rounding, narrowing or clamping. For V3, prefer a typed `float32` carrier; a `float64` supplied to V3 must be rejected unless *exactly representable* as float32 **including signed zero and underflow** (and with no NaN/Inf). For V4, preserve float64 without conversion. No integer-to-float lossy conversion silently: any accepted source type must be documented and exactly representable.
- **Signed-zero bit preservation:** +0 and -0 differ on the wire even though numerically equal; encode and compare their **raw IEEE-754 bit patterns** (e.g. `math.Float32bits`, `math.Float64bits`), not numeric `==` for echoed setpoints. Reject a response that changes the sign bit, swaps endianness, or changes any other echoed value bits, with UNKNOWN/quarantine and no blind retry.
- **Subnormals:** do not flush to zero; require bit-exact round trips for representative positive/negative minimum subnormal values and the transition around min-normal. Finite max/min must remain representable. Test overflow/inexact narrowing at the API boundary.
- **Statuses and safety:** preserve AO-002 F1–F6 and G41V2 invariants: one `0x28` record with count=1, strict length/index/variation/value/status/echo checks, error-IIN versus SUCCESS reconciliation, no false SUCCESS, no side effects on invalid inputs, unknown outcome quarantined, single-shot/no blind retry. Only Direct Operate **with response**. SBO, DirectOperateNoResponse, batches, other qualifiers, G42–G44, utility mappings or live control remain prohibited.
