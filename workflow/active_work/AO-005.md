Prepare the library's G41 analog-output control path for later owner consideration, without advertising it as supported yet:
- G41V1 signed int32, G41V2 int16, G41V3 IEEE float32, G41V4 IEEE float64.
- **One indexed point per request; Direct Operate WITH response only.**
- Explicit per-point status and echoed index/group/variation/value validation; no false SUCCESS.
- Reject malformed/unsupported qualifiers, count, variation, payload lengths, out-of-range indices and corrupted frames.
- Unknown control outcome after send/timeout/disconnect must fail closed; no blind retransmission or false acknowledgment.
- Keep exact-bit float echo checks; `-0.0` and smallest-subnormal peer echo normalization remain FAIL/DIVERGENCE and an explicit candidate interoperability exclusion, not a reason to weaken parsing.

Implement any G41 exercise behind an explicit, non-default experimental/test-only gate if required to reconcile current `Operate` admission with the published Reject contract. Do not expose success by default.
- No SBO, DirectOperateNoResponse, G41 via WRITE, multi-point, live SCADA/NGCP commands, or known mapping/scale assumptions.
